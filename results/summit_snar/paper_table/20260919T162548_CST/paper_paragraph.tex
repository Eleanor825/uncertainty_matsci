On the deterministic Summit SnAr reaction benchmark, we completed seven arms with five paired evaluation seeds and 50 oracle queries per trajectory (1,750 held-out queries), following a shared 550-query adaptation prior. Table~\ref{tab:snar-main} reports incremental hypervolume over this prior. Scalarized GP-EI achieved the largest mean final gain. The full method did not improve on the Qwen baseline: its paired mean final-gain difference was $-3.0408\times10^{-5}$ (two wins, one tie, and two losses). Both ES branches selected the original G0 checkpoint by the prespecified development rule; full/UQ-only and ES-only/baseline therefore produced identical per-seed HV curves. These results show a limitation of the current uncertainty-guided planner and do not demonstrate a selected-weight ES benefit. Variances quantify evaluation-seed variation conditional on one fixed adaptation run, not independently retrained models or unseen reaction functions.
